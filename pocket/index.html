            How Pocket works

            1. Add income — paycheck or extra cash lands in Ready to place.
            2. Split now — apply your % plan, split evenly, or type amounts into jars.
            3. Spend only from a jar — Pocket blocks the spend if that jar is empty or the amount is too high.
            4. Move money — shift leftover cash between jars without leaving Pocket.
